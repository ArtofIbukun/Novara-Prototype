Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Your privacy matters to us. Novara is built on a simple principle: your travel data belongs to you. We collect only what's necessary to provide the service, never sell your data, and give you full control over your information.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

When you create a Novara account, we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Travel logs: countries, cities, and regions you've visited
  • Trip names, dates, and notes you add

Information collected automatically

When you use Novara, we automatically collect:

  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Usage analytics (which features you use, session length)
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics
  • IP address and general location (country-level only)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Novara app and its features
  • Sync your travel data across your devices
  • Enable the Friends & Compare features when you opt in
  • Send you important account and service updates
  • Improve Novara through aggregated, anonymised analytics
  • Respond to your support requests

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • With friends you invite: Your username, profile photo, and travel percentage are visible to friends you explicitly connect with.
  • Service providers: We use trusted third-party providers for hosting, analytics, and push notifications. They are bound by strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of Novara and its users.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on servers located in the European Union. We use industry-standard encryption (TLS in transit, AES-256 at rest) and conduct regular security audits.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we permanently delete your personal data within 30 days.

5. Your Rights

Under GDPR and applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — update or correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and data
  • Portability — export your travel data in a standard format
  • Objection — object to certain processing activities

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@novara.app or use the settings in the app under Account → Privacy.

6. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies for authentication and preferences. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some website features may not function correctly.

7. Children's Privacy

Novara is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email or in-app notification at least 14 days before they take effect. Continued use of Novara after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact:

  • Email: privacy@novara.app
  • Data Controller: Novara Ltd, 1 Travel Square, London, EC1A 1BB, United Kingdom
